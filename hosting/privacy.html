Let's Spill privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Let's Spill ("the app") is an anonymous place to vent, rant and read confessions. This policy explains what we collect and why. Contact: ayushi.pandey@kitab.com.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your Google account ID and email address through Firebase Authentication. Your email is never shown to other readers; you appear under an anonymous name.
  • Profile: your anonymous name, age range (13 to 17, 18 to 24 and so on), chosen interests, join date and app settings.
  • Content you create: confessions you post, plus likes, reactions (Same, Hugs, Wow), saves and reports. Posts are public in the app but are not linked to your identity for other readers. We keep a private link from each post to its author so you can manage or delete it and so we can enforce our guidelines.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase records screens viewed and in-app actions (for example "confession opened") together with device and app information. It never includes confession text, search words, names or emails. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy > Usage analytics.
  • Push notifications: if you allow notifications, OneSignal receives a device push token and an identifier so we can send them. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone settings.

We do not collect your advertising ID, location, contacts, photos or microphone. The app shows no ads and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

To run the app (sign-in, showing and saving posts, personalising your feed), to keep it safe (reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our guidelines, hiding posts marked 18+ from readers under 18), and to understand which features help so we can improve them.

Who processes it

Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase) and OneSignal (push notifications) act as service providers. Data is encrypted in transit.

Teen readers

The app is for people aged 13 and over. Readers who choose the 13 to 17 age range never see posts marked 18+. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings > Delete account) or by following these steps. Deleting removes your profile, posts, likes, reactions, saves and sign-in record. Analytics data is anonymous and expires under Google Analytics retention settings.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, or ask questions, by emailing ayushi.pandey@kitab.com.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes and revise the date above.
