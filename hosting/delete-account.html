Delete your Let's Spill account

You can delete your Let's Spill account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Let's Spill and go to the You tab.
  2. Open Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm, then confirm with your Google account.

Without the app

Email ayushi.pandey@kitab.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Let's Spill account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile (anonymous name, age range, interests, settings), every confession you posted, your likes, reactions and saves, and your sign-in record. Reports you filed about other posts are kept for moderation records. Anonymous analytics that cannot identify you expire under Google Analytics retention settings.
